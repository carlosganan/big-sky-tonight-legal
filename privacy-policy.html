2. What we collect

When you sign in

On iPhone, Big Sky Live uses Sign in with Apple. When you sign in, Apple shares with us a unique Apple user identifier, an email address (which may be an Apple private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email") and, the first time only, the first name you allow Apple to share. We use the first name only to suggest a display name; you can change it.

On Android, Big Sky Live uses Sign in with Google. When you sign in, Google shares with us a unique Google Account identifier, your email address, your name and a link to your Google profile picture. We use your first name only to suggest a display name, which you can change; we do not use or show your Google profile picture.

If you sign in with Apple and with Google using the same verified email address, both sign-ins open the same Big Sky Live account.

You can browse the app without signing in. Signing in is required to post, reply, join rides or activities, and use other interactive features.

Your profile

Your display name and the avatar you pick. Both are public.

Device identifier

The app generates a random identifier on your phone the first time it runs. It is not tied to your Apple ID, Google Account, phone number or any advertising identifier, and it tells us nothing about who you are. We use it to keep your profile and your content together, and to apply rate limits.

Age range

To comply with children's privacy law, the app asks for your age once. On recent iPhones it first asks Apple's Declared Age Range service, which tells us only an age range that you or your parent have already declared to Apple. If that is not available, and always on Android, the app asks for your date of birth, calculates your age on your phone, and discards the date.

We store only whether you are an adult (18 or over) or a minor (13 to 17), and how that was determined. We never store or transmit your date of birth. If you are under 13, the app stops on your phone; see Section 6.

Agreements

The version of the Terms of Use you accepted and when, and when you acknowledged the activity risk notice. We keep these so we can show that you agreed.

Content you post

Town Board posts and replies, rides, activities, community events (title, place, date, time and details), Lost & Found items, road reports and photos. All of this is public inside the app. For Housing posts, the optional details you fill in (rent, bedrooms, availability, lease length, pets, furnished). The app does not ask for or show phone numbers; talk to other people in the post's thread.

For activities only, an organizer who is 18 or over may add an Instagram username. It is optional, and it is shown only to the organizer and to people 18 or over who have joined that activity. Users aged 13 to 17 cannot add an Instagram username and never see anyone else's.

Activity in the app

Reports you make about other people's content, events you mark "Going", threads you follow or mute, and which threads you have read. Reports are visible only to the developer.

Push notifications

If you turn notifications on, we store the push token assigned to your phone so we can deliver notifications: on iPhone it comes from Apple and Expo, and on Android from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo. The token identifies your phone for notifications only. We delete it when you sign out or delete your account. Notifications say only that there is a new reply in a thread (for example "New reply in Skis for sale"), never the name of the person who wrote it or the text of the message.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location on your phone, only while you are using the map, to show where you are. Your location is never sent to us or stored. On iPhone the map is Apple Maps; on Android it is Google Maps, which receives your phone's requests for map images under Google's own privacy policy.

People you block

Your block list is stored on your phone and also on our servers, linked to your profile, so that you do not get notifications about messages from people you have blocked. It is deleted when you unblock someone or delete your account.

Stored only on your phone

Your theme and other settings, and your signed-in session, are stored on your phone and are not sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, your precise location history, your advertising identifier, your payment information, or anything from other apps. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app and show your content to other neighbors.
  • To send push notifications you have asked for (for example, replies in threads you follow).
  • To moderate content, handle reports, keep people safe and prevent spam, including by limiting how often an account can post.
  • To apply the age-based limits described in the Terms of Use.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.
  • To comply with law and protect our rights.

We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to anyone.

4. What is public

Anyone using the app can see: your display name and avatar, everything you post (including photos), and your participation in rides and activities. The Instagram username an organizer adds to an activity is the only exception: only the organizer and people 18 or over who joined that activity can see it. Anyone 18 or over can join an activity, so treat it as visible to other adults in the app. Do not post anything you would not want other people in Big Sky to see, and never share personal details such as your phone number or address.

6. Children and teens

Big Sky Live is not for children under 13. The app asks for age before anything else and asks you to confirm the date you entered. If you are under 13, it stops there: no account is created and nothing is sent to us. The app remembers this only on that phone; deleting and reinstalling the app starts over. If you entered the wrong date, use "Made a mistake? Contact us" on that screen or email atat01036@gmail.com.

If a phone that used an earlier version of the app (before accounts existed) declares an age under 13, the app asks us once to remove what was posted from that phone. That content is hidden right away and permanently deleted after 7 days; copies may remain in backups for up to 30 more days. If we learn in any other way that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, email atat01036@gmail.com.

Users aged 13 to 17 can use the app with limits: they cannot offer, request or join rides or message in ride conversations, cannot create Housing or Jobs posts, and cannot post community events. They can browse, post in the other Town Board categories, Lost & Found and Activities, reply in public conversations, post road reports and report content. They cannot add an Instagram username to an activity or see anyone else's. We do not use any engagement-maximizing techniques directed at minors.

7. How long we keep it

  • Lost & Found items: about 14 days.
  • Rides: until the ride date has passed, then about 30 days.
  • Road reports: a few hours, then removed.
  • Activities: until the activity date has passed.
  • Community events: until the event date has passed, then removed.
  • Reports about content: about 90 days.
  • Your account, profile, Town Board posts and replies: until you delete them or your account.
  • Push tokens: until you sign out or delete your account.
  • People you block: until you unblock them or delete your account.
  • Content hidden after an under-13 notice from an earlier version of the app: 7 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Report emails: in the developer's mailbox until deleted; the copy queued on our servers for sending is removed after 30 days.

When you delete your account, everything listed in the app's deletion screen is removed, and we ask Apple to disconnect the app from your Apple ID. If you signed in with Google, the app removes its access to your Google Account before your account is deleted. Reports that other users made about your content may be kept for moderation, together with a short snapshot of the reported content. Technical logs kept by our service providers may hold your account identifier for a limited time, and some content may remain in backups for up to 30 days before it is purged.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account, or by email without the app. See how to delete your account for the steps and what is deleted.
  • Delete individual posts you have made from the post itself.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's Settings or in the app.
  • Block anyone from their post or profile.
  • Get a copy of your information, or correct or delete it: email atat01036@gmail.com (also under Settings → HELP → Contact us in the app). We will respond within 30 days.

If you do not have an account (for example, you posted with an earlier version of the app, before accounts existed, or you only used "Going" or road reports), you can still ask us to delete your information by email. Tell us what you posted and roughly when, so we can find it; we may ask you for details to confirm it is yours.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are performance of our agreement with you (the Terms of Use), our legitimate interest in running a safe community app, and your consent where we ask for it.

9. Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with a provider that uses industry-standard security. Access to data is restricted by database rules so that users can only change their own content. We do not store passwords (Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google do not give us one), and we never store your date of birth. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Each version has a version number and date at the top. When a change is material, the app will ask you to review and accept it again. Continued use after a non-material change means you accept the updated policy.
