Privacy Policy

Big Sky Live · Last updated: September 5, 2026

Big Sky Live (formerly "Big Sky Tonight"; "the app", "we", "us") is a free community app for Big Sky, Montana that helps locals and visitors discover local events, find people for activities, share rides, post lost & found notices, share road conditions anonymously, get safety information, and keep a personal log of the wildlife they spot. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • We do not require an account, name, email, or login to use the app.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or tracking.
  • The only information stored on our servers is the content you choose to post (RSVPs, rides, activities, lost & found notices, road reports and any photo you attach) and an anonymous, random device identifier used to make features work.

Information the app handles

  • Anonymous device identifier. The first time you open the app, a random identifier is generated and stored on your device. It is not your name, phone number, or Apple ID, and it is not linked to your real identity. It lets the app remember which posts are yours (so you can edit or delete them), keep RSVP counts consistent, and apply blocks and reports.
  • Content you post. When you RSVP to an event, post a ride, organize an activity, or create a lost & found notice, the information you enter is stored so other users can see it. This may include a display name you type, a title/description, dates and times, a meeting place, and a way to contact you: a phone number (required for rides and lost & found) and/or an Instagram handle (activities require at least one of the two). You choose whether the phone number opens SMS or WhatsApp. Road reports are anonymous: they contain only the road segment, the type of incident, an optional location and note, and no name or contact details.
  • Photos. Only lost & found notices can include a photo, chosen from your library or taken with the camera. The photo is resized (max 1200 px), converted to JPEG, and stored with the notice. Please do not include other people's faces, license plates, or personal documents in photos.
  • Avatar. You can design an optional cartoon avatar assembled from preset shapes and colors — never a photo of you. It is stored on your device and, as a short code, alongside the posts you publish.
  • Wildlife log. The animals you mark as spotted in the Wildlife section, and any reward you unlock, are stored only on your device. Nothing about your wildlife log is sent to our servers.
  • Reports. When you report a post, we store the reason you give, your anonymous device identifier, and a short copy of the reported post so we can review it even if the author edits or deletes it.
  • Consent records. When you accept the community rules shown before entering a posting section, the app stores on your device that you accepted them and when. Blocks you make are also stored only on your device.
  • Location. If you allow it, the app uses your device location while you are using the app only to show your position on the map. Your location is not stored on our servers or shared with anyone; you can decline the permission and still use the rest of the app.

We do not collect your contacts, browsing history, health data, or precise background location, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

How the information is used

Solely to operate the app's features: showing community posts to other users, letting you manage your own posts, keeping RSVP counts, calculating the status of a road from recent neighbor reports, and enabling safety tools (reporting and blocking). We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

How contact between users works

The app has no in-app messaging. When you tap "Contact" on a post, your phone opens your own Messages, WhatsApp, or Instagram app using the phone number or handle the poster chose to share. Any conversation from that point happens in those third-party apps under their own terms and privacy policies — not inside Big Sky Live. The Eat section offers "Call" buttons that open your phone app to call a venue, and "Call"/"Text" buttons to contact the developer; the app never sees those calls or messages. Safety & Alerts can open a pre-filled text message to 888777 that signs you up for the Big Sky Fire Department's own alert system; that subscription is handled entirely by the Fire Department.

Where your posts are stored

Posts, photos, and RSVP counts are stored using Supabase, a hosted database and storage provider that keeps the data on our behalf in the United States. We do not share your posts with any other third party. Venue, event, and menu content shown in the app is maintained by us and does not include your personal data; venue photos are loaded from Google Drive.

Retention and deletion

  • Posts are temporary by design and expire automatically: rides 30 days after their date, activities as soon as their date and time pass, lost & found notices 14 days after posting, and road reports about 6 hours after posting (each confirmation by another neighbor extends them briefly). Report records are kept for 90 days for moderation purposes and then deleted.
  • You can delete any post you created at any time from within the app; its photo is deleted with it. Photos attached to notices that expire automatically are removed from storage periodically.
  • Because there is no account, the way to remove your data is to delete your posts. If you would like anything else removed, contact us at the address below and we will help.

Safety and moderation

Before you can enter a posting section you must accept our community rules. You can report any post and block any user from within the app; blocked users' content is hidden on your device. Free-text fields are checked automatically against a list of offensive words in English and Spanish before a post is published. We review reports and remove content that violates our terms, typically within 24 hours, and we may restrict users who abuse the service. See our Terms of Use for our zero-tolerance policy on objectionable content.

Road reports and safety information

Road conditions in the app are anonymous reports from neighbors, not official information, and can be wrong or out of date. Safety & Alerts provides fixed information and links to official sources (for example the Big Sky Fire Department website); in an emergency always call 911.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information the app handles, including access controls on our database and storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and posts you publish are visible to other users of the app by design.

Your choices

  • Decline the location, camera, or photo library permissions (the app still works).
  • Choose not to include a phone number, Instagram handle, or photo in a post.
  • Delete your posts at any time, and block or report others.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
