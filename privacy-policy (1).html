1. Who we are and how to contact us

Big Sky Live is operated by Carlos Gañán Álvarez, Big Sky, Montana, USA. For any question about this policy or your information, email atat01036@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

When you sign in

Big Sky Live uses Sign in with Apple. When you sign in, Apple shares with us a unique Apple user identifier, an email address (which may be an Apple private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email") and, the first time only, the first name you allow Apple to share. We use the first name only to suggest a display name; you can change it.

You can browse the app without signing in. Signing in is required to post, reply, join rides or activities, and use other interactive features.

Your profile

Your display name and the avatar you pick. Both are public.

Device identifier

The app generates a random identifier on your phone the first time it runs. It is not tied to your Apple ID, phone number or any advertising identifier, and it tells us nothing about who you are. We use it to keep your profile and your content together, and to apply rate limits.

Age range

To comply with children's privacy law, the app asks for your age once. On recent iPhones it first asks Apple's Declared Age Range service, which tells us only an age range that you or your parent have already declared to Apple. If that is not available, the app asks for your date of birth, calculates your age on your phone, and discards the date.

We store only whether you are an adult (18 or over) or a minor (13 to 17), and how that was determined. We never store or transmit your date of birth. If you are under 13, the app stops before any information leaves your phone.

Agreements

The version of the Terms of Use you accepted and when, and when you acknowledged the activity risk notice. We keep these so we can show that you agreed.

Content you post

Town Board posts and replies, rides, activities, Lost & Found items, road reports, photos, and any phone number or contact method you choose to add to a post. All of this is public inside the app. For Housing posts, the optional details you fill in (rent, bedrooms, availability, lease length, pets, furnished).

Activity in the app

Reports you make about other people's content, events you mark "Going", threads you follow or mute, and which threads you have read. Reports are visible only to the developer.

Push notifications

If you turn notifications on, we store the push token Apple and Expo assign to your phone so we can deliver notifications. We delete it when you sign out, turn notifications off or delete your account.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location on your phone to show where you are on the map. Your location is never sent to us or stored.

Stored only on your phone

The people you have blocked, your theme and other settings, and your signed-in session are stored on your phone and are not sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, your precise location history, your advertising identifier, your payment information, or anything from other apps. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app and show your content to other neighbors.
  • To send push notifications you have asked for (for example, replies in threads you follow).
  • To moderate content, handle reports, keep people safe and prevent spam, including by limiting how often an account can post.
  • To apply the age-based limits described in the Terms of Use.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.
  • To comply with law and protect our rights.

We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to anyone.

4. What is public

Anyone using the app can see: your display name and avatar, everything you post (including photos and any contact details you add), and your participation in rides and activities. Do not post anything you would not want other people in Big Sky to see.

6. Children and teens

Big Sky Live is not for children under 13. The app asks for age before anything else and, if you are under 13, it stops there: no account is created and no information is sent to us. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, email atat01036@gmail.com.

Users aged 13 to 17 can use the app with limits: they cannot offer, request or join rides, and cannot post in Housing or Jobs. We do not use any engagement-maximizing techniques directed at minors.

7. How long we keep it

  • Lost & Found items: about 14 days.
  • Rides: until the ride date has passed, then about 30 days.
  • Road reports: a few hours, then removed.
  • Activities: until the activity date has passed.
  • Reports about content: about 90 days.
  • Your account, profile, Town Board posts and replies: until you delete them or your account.
  • Push tokens: until you sign out, turn notifications off or delete your account.

When you delete your account, everything listed in the app's deletion screen is removed. Some content may remain in backups for a short period before it is purged.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account.
  • Delete individual posts you have made from the post itself.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's Settings or in the app.
  • Block anyone from their post or profile.
  • Access or correct your information by emailing atat01036@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are performance of our agreement with you (the Terms of Use), our legitimate interest in running a safe community app, and your consent where we ask for it.

9. Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with a provider that uses industry-standard security. Access to data is restricted by database rules so that users can only change their own content. We do not store passwords (Sign in with Apple has none), and we never store your date of birth. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Each version has a version number and date at the top. When a change is material, the app will ask you to review and accept it again. Continued use after a non-material change means you accept the updated policy.
