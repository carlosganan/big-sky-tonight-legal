Privacy Policy

Big Sky Tonight · Last updated: September 1, 2026

Big Sky Tonight ("the app", "we", "us") is a free, community app for Big Sky, Montana that helps locals and visitors discover local events, find people for activities, share rides, and post lost & found notices. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • We do not require an account, name, email, or login to use the app.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or tracking.
  • The only information stored is the content you choose to post (events you RSVP to, rides, activities, or lost & found notices) and an anonymous, random device identifier used to make features work.

Information the app handles

  • Anonymous device identifier. The first time you open the app, a random identifier is generated and stored on your device. It is not your name, phone number, or Apple ID, and it is not linked to your real identity. It lets the app remember which posts are yours (so you can edit or delete them) and keep RSVP counts consistent.
  • Content you post. When you RSVP to an event, post a ride, organize an activity, or create a lost & found notice, the information you enter is stored so other users can see it. This may include a display name you type, a title/description, dates and times, a meeting place, and — only if you choose to add it — a phone number and/or Instagram handle so others can contact you. If the app lets you attach a photo to a post, that photo is stored as well.
  • Location. If you allow it, the app uses your device location while you are using the app only to show your position on the map. Your location is not stored on our servers or shared with anyone; you can decline the permission and still use the rest of the app.

We do not collect your contacts, browsing history, health data, or precise background location, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

How the information is used

Solely to operate the app's features: showing community posts to other users, letting you manage your own posts, keeping RSVP counts, and enabling safety tools (reporting and blocking). We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

How contact between users works

The app has no in-app messaging. When you tap "Contact" on a post, your phone opens your own Messages, WhatsApp, or Instagram app using the phone number or handle the poster chose to share. Any conversation from that point happens in those third-party apps under their own terms and privacy policies — not inside Big Sky Tonight.

Where your posts are stored

Posts and RSVP counts are stored using Supabase, a hosted database and storage provider that keeps the data on our behalf. We do not share your posts with any other third party. Content shown in the app (venues, events, menus) is maintained by us and does not include your personal data.

Retention and deletion

  • Posts are temporary by design and expire automatically (for example, rides are removed after their departure time and other posts after their date passes).
  • You can delete any post you created at any time from within the app.
  • Because there is no account, the way to remove your data is to delete your posts. If you would like anything else removed, contact us at the address below and we will help.

Safety and moderation

You can report any post and block any user from within the app; blocked users' content is hidden on your device. We review reports and remove content that violates our terms, and we may remove content or restrict users who abuse the service. See our Terms of Use for our zero-tolerance policy on objectionable content.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information the app handles, including access controls on our database. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and posts you publish are visible to other users of the app by design.

Your choices

  • Decline the location permission (the app still works).
  • Choose not to include a phone number or Instagram handle in a post.
  • Delete your posts at any time, and block or report others.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
