Delete your Big Sky Live account

Big Sky Live · Carlos Gañán Álvarez · Last updated October 12, 2026

Big Sky Live is a free community app for Big Sky, Montana, operated by Carlos Gañán Álvarez. You can delete your account and the information connected to it at any time, on iPhone or Android, with or without the app installed.

Fastest way: in the app, open your avatar (top left) → Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

1. Delete it in the app

  1. Open Big Sky Live and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap your avatar in the top left corner, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account. The screen lists everything that will be deleted.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete my account.

Your account and the information below are deleted right away. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to disconnect the app from your Apple ID. If you signed in with Google, the app removes its access to your Google Account before deleting.

2. Request it by email (no app needed)

If you no longer have the app, send an email to atat01036@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Big Sky Live account" and include:

  • The email address of your account, and how you signed in (Apple, Google or email).
  • If you used "Hide My Email" with Apple, the name shown on your profile in the app, so we can find your account.

Please write from the email address of your account when you can. To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request from that address before deleting anything. We will confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days of receiving your request.

3. What is deleted

  • Your profile: name and avatar.
  • Your Town Board posts, replies and messages in every conversation.
  • Rides, activities, Lost & Found items and neighbor events you posted, and their conversations.
  • Road reports, "Going" marks and reports you made.
  • Photos you uploaded.
  • Your notification token, so you stop getting notifications.
  • Your sign-in (Apple, Google or email) and your age and terms records.

4. What may be kept, and for how long

We do not keep anything else after your account is deleted, and we do not sell or share your information.

5. Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Posts, rides, activities, items and messages: delete any of them from the item itself in the app.
  • "Going" marks: tap the event again to remove it.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings; your notification token is removed when you sign out.
  • Anything else: email atat01036@gmail.com and tell us what you want removed. We will respond within 30 days.
